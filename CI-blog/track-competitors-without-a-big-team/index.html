Track Competitors Without a Big Team

Mid-market manufacturers need competitive intelligence, but rarely have the headcount or budget for a dedicated in-house team. Here's how to get strategic insights without the overhead.

Short answer: The most effective way for mid-market manufacturers to track competitors without a big team is to outsource their competitive intelligence function to a specialized consultancy. This provides access to advanced AI pipelines, multi-source data collection, and expert analysis at a fraction of the cost of building an in-house team.

Most mid-market manufacturing leaders know they need competitive intelligence. Their markets are shifting, new competitors are emerging from unexpected places, and strategic decisions require real-time data.

But they hit a wall:

  • A dedicated CI analyst costs $120K-$180K/year (fully loaded).
  • CI software platforms are $15K-$30K/year, but are often built for SaaS, not manufacturing.
  • Building an in-house methodology takes 6-12 months of trial and error.

The solution isn't to compromise on intelligence. It's to optimize the delivery model.

What to Look for in an Outsourced CI Partner

Not all CI consultancies are equal. Demand these three things:
