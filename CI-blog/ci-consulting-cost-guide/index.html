CI Consulting: Cost and How to Choose the Right Partner

A transparent guide to what competitive intelligence consulting costs, what you get at each price point, and how to evaluate providers.

The one-sentence version: CI consulting for manufacturing ranges from $10,000 to $60,000/month for outsourced solutions, with custom AI-powered pipelines and weekly analyst deliverables. The biggest cost drivers are coverage breadth and delivery frequency. Avoid consultants who don't specialise in manufacturing — the signal model is everything.

Three levels of engagement, from starter to enterprise

Cost clarity: These ranges assume outsourced CI as a managed service. In-house CI teams add salary, tools, and infrastructure costs — typically $150K–$300K/year for a single analyst, before any data subscriptions.
Slide 2 of 3

What Drives Cost

Three factors explain 90% of the variance

🌐 Scope Complexity

The number of markets, product categories, and geographies tracked. Broader coverage requires more infrastructure and analyst attention.

📡 Signal Diversity

A system tracking patents, certifications, tenders, and hiring costs more than one tracking only pricing and news. Manufacturing-relevant signals require specialised data pipelines.

⏱️ Delivery Frequency

Weekly reports are standard. Continuous monitoring (real-time alerts + dashboards) and monthly deep-dive teardowns increase cost but deliver substantially more strategic value.

✅ What You're Really Paying For

Not data — data is cheap. You're paying for signal verification, analyst judgement, and manufacturing domain expertise. That's where the value lives.

How to Choose a CI Partner

Four questions to ask before signing

🏭 "Do you specialise in manufacturing?"

If they say "we cover all industries," walk away. Manufacturing data sources (patents, cert registries, tender platforms) are fundamentally different from SaaS or retail.

📋 "What signal types do you track?"

Look for patents, certifications, tenders, and hiring — not just news and pricing. If they can't name the databases they monitor, they're reselling generic feeds.

🔒 "How do you ensure reliability?"

Demand a clear methodology for distinguishing verified signals from noise. At GEODRIV, we use a three-tier confidence framework with auditable data sources.

👤 "Who will I work with?"

You want direct access to the analyst who understands your industry, not a salesperson who forwards your questions to an anonymous back-office team.
