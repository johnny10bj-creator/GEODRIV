What Is GEODRIV?

GEODRIV builds AI-powered competitive intelligence systems for manufacturing companies that need to see what their competitors are doing — before the market shifts.

is a competitive intelligence consultancy for manufacturing. We design custom CI systems that combine automated signal collection from 7+ data channels with human analyst synthesis — delivering weekly intelligence that turns raw data into decisive action.

Manufacturing companies face a structural intelligence gap. They operate in markets where competitive signals are fragmented across patent databases, certification registries, tender platforms, hiring announcements, and industry news — but most CI tools were built for SaaS, where the data lives on web pages.

GEODRIV was founded to close that gap. We bring:

Our Methodology

🔍 Discovery

We map your competitive landscape: who competes, what signals matter, which data sources carry the highest signal-to-noise ratio for your specific segment.

📡 Multi-Source Collection

Automated pipelines across 7+ channels: patents, certifications, tenders, hiring, news, financials, and regulatory — purpose-built for manufacturing, not SaaS.

🧠 AI Synthesis

Our models cross-reference signals across channels to surface composite intelligence — a patent filing + a new hire + a new certification = a strategic signal no single tool would catch.

📋 Executive Delivery

Weekly intelligence briefings, competitor teardowns, and strategic alerts — structured for decisions, not for dashboards.

Who We Work With

Mid-market to enterprise manufacturers in:

  • Industrial automation and robotics
  • Electronics manufacturing (EMS/ODM)
  • Medical device and precision components
  • Automotive and EV supply chain
  • Advanced materials and processing

Why GEODRIV?

Because most CI systems are built for the wrong game. If you're tracking competitors who move on certification changes, tender wins, and patent filings — not feature releases and pricing pages — you need a system designed for that reality.

We've been doing this for 15 years. We know what signal patterns precede a market disruption, and we've built the automation to catch them before they hit your RFQ inbox.
