CI Consulting: Cost and How to Choose the Right Partner

A transparent guide to what competitive intelligence consulting costs, what you get at each price point, and how to evaluate providers.

Short answer: CI consulting for manufacturing ranges from $10,000 to $60,000/month for outsourced solutions, with custom AI-powered pipelines and weekly analyst deliverables. The biggest cost drivers are coverage breadth and delivery frequency (weekly reports vs. continuous monitoring). Avoid consultants who don't specialize in manufacturing — the signal model is everything.

What Drives Cost

The price of CI consulting isn't arbitrary. Three factors explain 90% of the variance:

  • Scope complexity — The number of markets, product categories, and geographies being tracked. Broader coverage requires more infrastructure and analyst attention.
  • Signal diversity — A system tracking patents, certifications, tenders, and hiring costs more than one tracking only pricing and news. Manufacturing-relevant signals (patents, certs) often require specialized data pipelines.
  • Delivery frequency — Weekly reports are standard. Continuous monitoring (real-time alerts + dashboards) and monthly deep-dive teardowns increase cost but deliver substantially more strategic value.

How to Choose a CI Partner

Four questions to ask any CI consultant before signing:

  1. "Do you specialize in manufacturing?" — If they say "we cover all industries," walk away. The data sources that matter in manufacturing are fundamentally different from SaaS or retail.
  2. "What signal types do you track?" — Look for patents, certifications, tenders, and hiring — not just news and pricing. If they can't name the specific databases they monitor, they're reselling generic feeds.
  3. "How do you ensure reliability?" — Demand a clear methodology for distinguishing verified signals from noise. At GEODRIV, we use a three-tier confidence framework with auditable data sources.
  4. "Who will I work with?" — You want direct access to the analyst who understands your industry, not a salesperson who forwards your questions to an anonymous back-office team.
