The Real Cost of Building an In-House CI Team

Most mid-market manufacturers spend $200K+ on an internal CI function that delivers less than a $3K/month outsourced system. Here's the math.

Short answer: Hiring one CI analyst costs $120K-$180K/year fully loaded. They still need tools ($15K-$30K), data subscriptions ($5K-$10K), and 6-12 months to build methodology. A specialized outsourced CI system delivers the same intelligence for $36K-$96K/year — with no ramp-up time, broader coverage, and manufacturing-specific methodology already in place.

Now compare to outsourced CI: $3K – $8K/month = $36K – $96K/year. Zero ramp-up. Manufacturing-specific signals. Weekly deliverables. No management overhead.

What You Actually Get

An in-house CI analyst, no matter how capable, can reasonably monitor 5-15 competitors across 3-4 signal types. They spend 40-60% of their time on collection and synthesis, leaving limited capacity for strategic analysis.

An outsourced CI system like GEODRIV covers 20-50+ competitors across 7+ signal types. Automated collection frees the human analyst (you or your team) for strategic interpretation.

The Hidden Cost: Missed Signals

The biggest cost of in-house CI isn't salary — it's the competitor move that nobody caught because one person can't watch everything. A patent filing in a foreign jurisdiction. A certification change at a supplier that signals a new entrant into your segment. A tender award that reveals a competitor's pricing strategy.

Single-person CI functions inevitably have blind spots. A system designed for manufacturing, with automated multi-channel monitoring, doesn't.
