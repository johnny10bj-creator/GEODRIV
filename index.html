Illustrative decision brief

How should an expansion signal be verified?

Scenario: A manufacturer wants to understand whether a competitor is entering a new regional market.

Observed signals

  • Public job listings include new regional sales roles.
  • Public procurement notices indicate additional equipment needs.
  • One signal does not prove that capacity is operational or market entry is complete.

Possible implications

The signals may indicate expansion preparations, staff replacement or an upgrade to an existing production line. More sources are needed to assess them.

Next steps to verify

Verify listing dates and locations; investigate equipment use and project milestones; compare new evidence in the next brief.
