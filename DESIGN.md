# Geodriv public website design system

Current direction approved through the October 2026 homepage reference: white navigation, closed blue G symbol, navy Earth hero and editorial content sections. This supersedes the older Industrial Brief specification.

## Typography
- Homepage and industry pages: Inter for English, Noto Sans SC for Chinese.
- Existing article and contact templates may retain Lexend and Source Sans 3 while sharing the same brand, accessible navigation and colour direction.
- Inter is allowed. Do not change approved layouts solely to satisfy superseded font rules.

## Identity and colours
- Public wordmark, titles, bylines and publisher metadata: Geodriv.
- Registered legal name: 上海爵领科技有限公司.
- Symbol: assets/geodriv-mark.svg, the continuous-circle G mark.
- Public homepage tokens: white #fff, ink #0c1935, action blue #064bba, hero navy #02142d, panels #f2f6fb.
- Dark theme tokens remain in assets/home.css. Use its responsive rules as the current implementation reference.

## Content and accessibility
- Use real article destinations, evidence-backed text and clearly distinguished facts and interpretations.
- Preserve keyboard focus, semantic headings, readable contrast and responsive layouts.
- Buttons and navigation labels describe user actions; internal tool administration is excluded from the public site.
- robots and noindex are discovery controls, not access control.
